## ⭐ Top pick (high-priority this run)

### 1. On-Device Content Safety API
A one-line, calibrated multi-category moderation classifier (hate, harassment, sexual, violence, self-harm, spam) that runs on-device and never sends user content to the cloud.
- **Type:** G (Trust, safety & compliance) + Type F reach · **Verdict:** Prototype (developer preview) · **Opportunity: 7/10 — Strong, gated**
- **Why it's the pick:** strongest demand of the set (8+ cloud vendors charging ~$1–3/1k; nsfwjs 8,997★; DSA + UK OSA make moderation mandatory), genuine whitespace (no vendor ships a standalone on-device moderation *API* — ShieldGemma/Llama Guard are weights-only, every rich moderator is cloud), and feasible at wide reach (small model, ~96% of devices, not Nano-gated).
- **Tags:** `whitespace` `regulatory-clock` `install-weighted` `wide-reach` · **Watch:** durability flag — Google is sunsetting its own Perspective API after Dec 2026.
